# ReceiptBrain

**A receipt scanner that never phones home.**

Photograph a receipt and the merchant, amount, date and category are read on the
device. No account, no cloud, no subscription — and no network calls at all.

## What it does

- **Scans with the system document camera**, which corrects perspective and
  contrast before the text is read
- **Reads on-device** through Apple's Vision framework, in your phone's language
- **Sorts by category** — groceries, dining, transport — from the merchant
- **Monthly totals** with charts, search across history, export
- **A widget** with this month's spending
- **Works in airplane mode**, because it never needed the network

## Private by construction

Nothing is uploaded. There is no analytics, no crash reporter, no advertising
identifier, no third-party SDK. Your receipts are on your phone and, if you use
iCloud, in your own iCloud account.
