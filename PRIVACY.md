# Privacy Policy — ReceiptBrain

**Last updated: 19 August 2026**

ReceiptBrain reads receipts on your iPhone and keeps them there. It has no
server, no account, and makes no network requests at all — there is nowhere for
your data to go.

## What stays on your device

The receipt photos, the text recognised from them, the merchant, amount, date
and category, and every total the app shows you. All of it lives in the app's
own storage on your phone.

Recognition runs on-device through Apple's Vision framework, and the parsing
that turns that text into a receipt runs on-device too — on newer iPhones
through Apple's built-in language model, which also never leaves the device.
No photo and no receipt is uploaded anywhere.

## What is sent

Nothing. The app makes no network calls. There is no analytics SDK, no crash
reporter, no advertising identifier, and no third-party code that phones home.
Turning the phone into airplane mode changes nothing about how the app works.

## iCloud

If you have iCloud enabled, your receipts sync through **your own** iCloud
account under Apple's terms, the same way your notes and photos do. We have no
access to it and no copy of it.

## Camera and photos

The camera is used to photograph a receipt, and the photo library is used if you
pick an existing photo instead. Both happen only when you tap to scan, and the
resulting image is stored in the app, not sent anywhere.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing that could identify
anyone.

## Deleting your data

Delete a receipt in the app, or delete the app — that removes everything. There
is nothing held elsewhere to request or erase.

## Changes

Material changes will appear here with a new date.
