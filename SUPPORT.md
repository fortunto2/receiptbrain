# Support — ReceiptBrain

Something broken or confusing? Write to
[info@superduperai.co](mailto:info@superduperai.co) — a person reads it.

For a bug, the useful details are: what you scanned, what the app showed, what
you expected, and the app version from Settings.

## Questions that come up

**A receipt scanned badly.** Use the in-app camera rather than an existing photo
where you can — it corrects perspective and contrast before the text is read.
Flatten the receipt, avoid shadow across it, and fill the frame. Long receipts
scan better in two goes than one distant shot.

**The total is wrong.** Tap the receipt and correct it; the correction sticks.
The app looks for a total keyword first and the largest amount second, which is
right on most receipts and wrong on a few unusual layouts.

**Which languages?** Recognition follows your phone's language. Turkish,
Russian, English, German, French, Spanish and Italian receipts are handled.

**Is anything uploaded?** No. The app makes no network calls at all — see the
[privacy policy](/privacy/).

**Where are my receipts if I delete the app?** Gone, unless iCloud sync had them.
Export first from the export screen if you want to keep them.
